Transforming a county government’s relationship with data does not require a multi-million-dollar technology overhaul. In fact, attempting to "boil the ocean" usually results in slow, expensive failure.

Instead, start small by changing executive micro-habits.
- **Change the Meeting Currency:** For your next agenda, require one piece of structured, operational data to justify the biggest decision on the table.
- **Redesign the Ask:** Mandate that all future requests for new headcount, equipment, or expanded programs be accompanied by a structured data narrative. If there is no data to prove the need, the request is not ready for review.

Finally, **embrace the mess.** The first time you pull your county's data into the light, it will be ugly, incomplete, and full of errors. That is not a sign of failure; it is step one. The organizations that succeed are the ones brave enough to look at the ugly data and begin cleaning it up, one fact at a time.
