A data-smart culture can begin with a change in leadership habits, not a procurement. Use this illustrative first-month plan as a discussion exercise.

| When | Leader's action | What to look for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First meeting | Require one operational fact for a major agenda decision. | The fact has a clear definition and time period. |
| Next discussion | Ask how the fact was collected and where it may be incomplete. | Staff can surface limitations without being blamed. |
| Next review | Identify who enters the information, who checks quality, and who interprets results. | Responsibilities are understood across teams. |
| End of month | Revisit one decision using a consistent measure. | The team can explain what changed and what remains uncertain. |

Treat an imperfect first report as the starting point for improvement. The immediate goal is not a perfect dashboard. It is a repeatable expectation that important decisions will be supported by evidence.
