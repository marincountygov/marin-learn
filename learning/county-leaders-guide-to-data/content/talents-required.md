Managing servers (IT) is fundamentally different from generating insights from information (Data). As you build this capability, you must source the right talent efficiently so you do not permanently inflate your headcount for temporary needs.

- **The Builders (Data Engineers):** These are the plumbers who build the secure pipes to move data from isolated department software into a central hub. This is heavy, highly technical lifting.
    - *Sourcing:* Primarily **Contractors or Specialized Vendors** for the initial architectural build. *If leveraging talented existing staff:* Do so, but plan strategically for their retention and eventual replacement. You must mandate that they adhere to industry standards, require rigorous documentation before launch, and ensure what they build aligns with what your long-term IT infrastructure can actually support.
- **The Interpreters (Data Analysts):** These are the translators who turn raw numbers into dashboards, maps, and answers for leadership.
    - *Sourcing:* **Internal Staff.** Analysts require deep, ongoing context regarding county operations, goals, and internal politics to know *what* questions to ask.
- **The Enforcers (Data Stewards):** These are the guardians of data hygiene at the point of entry.
    - *Sourcing:* **Existing Staff.** This is not a new hire; it is a shift in daily responsibility for current department heads, frontline managers, and clerks to ensure data is entered accurately.
